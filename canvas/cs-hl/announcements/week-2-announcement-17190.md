# Week 2 Announcement

Dear Students,

Welcome to Week 2!

As a reminder, our week begins on Wednesday and ends on Tuesday at 23:59 UK time.

This week, we are focusing on the CPU and its components, the GPU, primary memory, and the fetch, decode and execute cycle. This is a chance to understand what is actually happening inside a computer when you run a program or play a game. It also provides a foundation for understanding why some computers run faster than others.

There are several interactive tasks waiting for you, as well as two assignments. One is a graded test, and the other involves participation in a discussion.

For the discussion task, please note that you are expected not only to post your own response but also to interact meaningfully with your classmates. To allow enough time for a genuine exchange of ideas, please do not leave this task until the very last moment. By posting earlier, you give yourself and your classmates the opportunity to read, reflect on, and respond to each other's contributions.

I hope you enjoy this unit!
