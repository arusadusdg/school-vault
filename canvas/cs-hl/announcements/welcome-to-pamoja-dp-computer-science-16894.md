# Welcome to Pamoja DP Computer Science

**Dear Students,**

Welcome to Pamoja Computer Science and to the new academic year.

My name is Anna Dabska, and I will be your Pamoja Computer Science teacher of record. I have been an IB teacher since 2004 and have taught Computer Science since 2007.

![image.png](https://pamojaeducation.instructure.com/courses/734/files/458300/preview?verifier=effe3a56-3e6f-4632-937f-cb5611bd4d25)

Over the years, I have also taught ICT, Design, English as a Second Language, working with learners of different ages, from 5-year-olds to adults. All of these areas have something in common I am passionate about: human ingenuity and curiosity.

I have also worked with the IB as an examiner and workshop leader, and I write for the InThinking Computer Science website.

Over the next two years, you will study the main areas of the IB Computer Science course through Theme A and Theme B. You will develop your understanding of computer systems, computational thinking, programming, problem solving, and the wider impact of computing. You will also work progressively towards your Internal Assessment, where you will apply your knowledge and skills in a practical project.

Your final assessment will include external examinations as well as the Internal Assessment. The exact structure will depend on whether you are studying SL or HL. We will discuss each assessment component, expectations, and strategies as we progress through the course, so there is no need to worry about understanding everything at the beginning.

Very soon, we will also have group meetings and one-to-one meetings. These will give us an opportunity to get to know each other better, discuss how you are settling into the course, and make sure you know where to find support when you need it.

For now, please take some time to explore Canvas and become familiar with Units, Assignments, Announcements, Feedback, Online Classroom, and Inbox. Our Pamoja week runs from Wednesday to Tuesday, and assignments are due by midnight Tuesday, UK time.

Online learning works best when we communicate regularly. Please stay engaged with the weekly learning, keep track of deadlines, and reach out whenever you need clarification or support. I will be here to guide you, provide feedback, and support your progress throughout the course.

I look forward to getting to know you and working with you over the coming two years.

Warm regards,

**Anna Dabska**

Trimbach, Switzerland | GMT+2
