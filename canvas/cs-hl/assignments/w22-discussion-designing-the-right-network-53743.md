# W22 - Discussion - Designing the Right Network

**Topics:** IPv4 and IPv6 addressing, public and private IP addresses, static and dynamic IP addresses, NAT, transmission media, packet switching, routers and switches.

**Scenario:**  
 A university is designing a campus network. It must support thousands of student devices, online learning systems, cloud applications, video conferencing and secure access to resources.

**Discussion Prompt:**  
 To what extent should organisations prioritise performance over cost when designing networks?

1. Compare IPv4 and IPv6 addressing and explain why organisations are moving towards IPv6.
2. Discuss the advantages and limitations of fibre optic, twisted pair and wireless transmission.
3. Explain how NAT allows private IP addresses to communicate with the public internet.
4. Explain how packet switching allows data to travel efficiently across networks.
5. Reach a supported conclusion considering performance, cost, reliability and security.

**Discussion Guidelines**

* Post one original response addressing all required points.
* Use appropriate computer science terminology and apply concepts to realistic situations.
* Reply meaningfully to at least two classmates.
* Meaningful replies should add technical reasoning, suggest alternatives or identify limitations.
* Simple agreement without explanation is not considered a meaningful contribution.
