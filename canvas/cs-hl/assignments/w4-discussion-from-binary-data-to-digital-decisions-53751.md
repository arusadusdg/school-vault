# W4 - Discussion: From Binary Data to Digital Decisions

## **Discussion prompt**

Modern computer systems store all data as binary and use combinations of logic gates to make decisions and process that data. Consider a smart security system that uses a camera, microphone, keypad and motion sensor. The system must store images and audio, recognize input, compare conditions and decide whether to unlock a door or activate an alarm.

In your initial post:

* Explain how two different types of data used by the security system are represented and stored in binary form.
* Discuss how logic gates could be combined to control one decision made by the system. Your response should refer to at least two named gates or Boolean operators.
* Evaluate the usefulness of hexadecimal notation when programmers inspect or debug the binary data used by the system. Consider both strengths and limitations before reaching a conclusion.

## **Exact participation requirements**

1. Submit one initial post of 250 to 350 words.
2. Address all three parts of the prompt in clearly separated paragraphs.
3. Use at least six relevant computer science terms accurately. Suitable terms include bit, byte, binary encoding, character encoding, pixel, colour depth, sampling rate, logic gate, Boolean operator, truth table and hexadecimal.
4. Include at least one specific example of binary or hexadecimal data, such as a short conversion, byte pattern or colour value.
5. Refer directly to the smart security system scenario throughout the response.
6. Reply to at least two classmates. Each reply should be 80 to 120 words.
7. Each reply must make a meaningful contribution by extending an idea, correcting or respectfully challenging a claim, adding a relevant example, or asking and answering a thoughtful follow-up question.
8. Replies that only state agreement, praise the post or repeat the original idea will not meet the requirement.
9. Use a respectful, academic tone and submit original work.
