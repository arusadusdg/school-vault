# W1 - Meet your teacher

It is a requirement for Year 1 students to have met with their teacher within the first four weeks of the course.

It is an introductory meeting for you to ‘meet’ and introduce yourself to the teacher with whom you will work for the duration of your two-year diploma course. This might be individually, with other students from your school who are in the same class, or part of a whole class online lesson.

Your teachers will reach out to you to arrange this meeting. It is expected that you are proactive in ensuring that this meeting takes place. If you are not timely in responding, then the teacher may copy in your Site Based Coordinator (SBC) to help facilitate setting up a meeting.

Students who have not met with their teacher, or who have not attempted to schedule a meeting, within the first four weeks of the course will have this noted on the report for the first set of Engagement Ratings.

This will be due as a task at the end of week four but may be marked completed by your teacher sooner.
