# W2 - The CPU at Work

**Discussion Prompt**

Throughout this week's lessons, you explored how the CPU carries out instructions using its internal components, how different types of primary memory support processing, and why modern computers increasingly rely on GPUs alongside CPUs.

Reflect on how these components work together to enable the performance of modern computing systems.

In your initial post, address the following:

* **Explain** how the CPU components (ALU, CU, registers and buses) work together during the fetch-decode-execute cycle.
* **Discuss** one real-world application (for example, artificial intelligence, gaming, scientific simulations, image processing or another example of your choice) where both the CPU and GPU play important roles. Explain why each processor is suited to its specific tasks.
* **Evaluate** the importance of cache memory in improving system performance. What impact would frequent cache misses have on a computer's performance?

Support your ideas with appropriate computer science terminology and, where possible, include examples from your own experiences or observations.

**Discussion Guidelines**

* Make one original post that fully addresses all parts of the discussion prompt.
* Use appropriate IB Computer Science terminology throughout your response.
* Where appropriate, support your ideas with examples or evidence.
* After posting your response, reply to **at least two classmates**.
* Your replies should make a **meaningful contribution** to the discussion. Rather than simply agreeing, you should:

+ ask thoughtful follow-up questions,
+ offer an alternative perspective,
+ build upon your classmate's explanation,
+ provide an additional example or application, or
+ respectfully challenge an idea using sound reasoning.

* Keep your responses professional, respectful, and constructive.
* Aim to contribute to a discussion that helps everyone deepen their understanding of the week's concepts.
