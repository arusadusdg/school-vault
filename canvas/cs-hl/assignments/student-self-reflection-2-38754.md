# ✏️ Student Self-Reflection 2

This task is an opportunity for you to comment on your progress in the course so far, and for your teachers to respond to you in a way that will help you develop your work further. To help you organize your self-reflection appropriately, you should focus on the following questions.

For each question below you should write at least a sentence. Your self-reflection as a whole should be between 80 -140 words.

**Engagement**: Have you spent enough time in the course and on the set work to be successful?

**Learning**: Tell us about what you have learned in your subject? What has been the most useful and interesting part of the course for you so far?

**Essential Skills**: How are you enjoying online learning in this subject? (Please consider your self-management, communication and interaction).

**Your Tasks**

* Complete your self-reflection and submit.
