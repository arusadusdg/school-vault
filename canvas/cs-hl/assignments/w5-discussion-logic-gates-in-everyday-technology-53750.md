# W5 - Discussion: Logic Gates in Everyday Technology

Logic gates are the fundamental building blocks of digital systems. Although they operate using simple binary inputs and outputs, their combinations enable computers, smartphones, medical devices, vehicles and many other technologies to make decisions and process information.

Explain one real-world application in which logic gates are essential. Discuss how combinations of logic gates make the system function, and to what extent simplifying Boolean expressions can improve the efficiency or reliability of the system. Support your ideas with appropriate computer science terminology and relevant examples.
