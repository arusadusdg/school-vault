**Scenario: University Campus Network**

A university is expanding its campus network. The network must support thousands of student devices, online learning systems, cloud applications, staff systems and secure access to university resources. The design team must consider reliability, scalability, performance, cost and security.

**Assessment Questions**

1. Describe two characteristics of a LAN. [2]
2. Explain the purpose of a router and a switch in a network. [2]
3. Compare star and mesh topologies considering reliability, scalability and cost. [2]
4. Compare client-server and peer-to-peer networking models. [2]
5. Distinguish between IPv4 and IPv6 addressing. [2]
6. Explain how NAT allows private IP addresses to communicate with the public internet. [2]
7. Evaluate fibre optic cable compared with wireless transmission for connecting university buildings. [3]
8. Discuss the effectiveness of firewalls in protecting a network. Include strengths and limitations. [4]
9. Explain the difference between symmetric and asymmetric encryption. [2]
10. Evaluate network vulnerabilities and suitable countermeasures. [4]
