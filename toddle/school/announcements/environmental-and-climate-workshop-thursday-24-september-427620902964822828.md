# Environmental and Climate Workshop – Thursday, 24 September

Dear DP students and parents,

Next ****Thursday, 24 September****, we will have a guest scientist at school, Dr. Giulio Settanta, who will lead a workshop on environmental and climate science for all our DP students. A description of the activity is provided below.

**The activity is aimed at school students and provides an overview of some of the activities carried out by ISPRA (Italian Institute for Environmental Protection and Research). Topics include climate and climate variation, adaptation to climate change, urban green infrastructure, and sustainable mobility. The activity includes both a presentation and an interactive part, during which students will be guided through the analysis of climate data.**

The schedule is as follows:

* ****Thursday, 24 September, from 9:00 to 11:30: all DP1 students****
* ****Thursday, 24 September, from 12:00 to 13:00 and from 13:45 to 15:15: all DP2 students****

****Attendance at the workshop is mandatory for**** ****all DP students.**** Students will be excused from the classes that overlap with the activity.

We are looking forward to this experience!

Best regards,

Ms Meraviglia
