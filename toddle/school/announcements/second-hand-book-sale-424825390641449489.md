# Second-Hand Book Sale

Dear Parents and Students,

We hope you had a wonderful summer break and are looking forward to the new academic year! 😊

Since many of you have contacted us with questions about books for the new academic year, we have some good news: second-hand books will be available for purchase during the Kick-Off Party on Friday, 18 September.

Students who would like to sell or donate their old books are also very welcome to bring them to the school office during Welcome Week. Please put a note inside each book with:

* your name
* the price
* your payment details

Thank you for helping us give the books a second life and making it easier for everyone to prepare for the new school year! 📚

We look forward to seeing you at the school!

Best regards,

Valentina
