# DP1 trip to Heidelberg - Thursday

Dear DP1 students,

The first day is already over! I was very happy to see you all again and to meet our new members.

It was a very productive day, and since we are on track with everything we need to complete by the end of this week, I think we all deserve to go out for a short trip around Heidelberg on Thursday morning (September 17).

The trip will only take up part of the morning, from 7:55 until around 11:00.

The plan is as follows:

* We meet at 7:55 in front of the school
* We go together to Bismarckplatz by tram (please bring your Deutschland ticket or regular tram ticket)
* We walk along the Neckarwiese towards the Old Bridge, and then walk back to Bismarckplatz via Hauptstrasse.
* From Bismarckplatz, we take the tram back to school.

From 11:00 to 13:00, we will finalize our doors' decorations and practice our learner profile performances for Friday afternoon.

I hope you enjoy the next two days at school, and I will see you all on Thursday!

Best

Ms. Meraviglia
