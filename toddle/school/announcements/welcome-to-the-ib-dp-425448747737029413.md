# Welcome to the IB DP

Dear new DP1 students,

welcome back to school and welcome to the beginning of your next journey: the IB Diploma Programme.

You should have all received the welcome information and package from your homeroom teacher Ms. Meraviglia in the last days. Please check that you can all log into Toddle before the welcome week. Previous MYP students can use their usual school email accounts to log in.   
Our new DP1 students can log into the system with their private email accounts and we will change them to the school email accounts in the course of the next week. Go to <https://www.toddleapp.com/>, click on "Sign in", use the student account, and if you have a GMAIL account, simply click on GMAIL and use your regular GMAIL data. If you cannot log in, do not worry and simply reach out to me. I can send you a one-time password and we will switch your accounts to the school email accounts anyways next week. On Toddle, you can already find your homeroom class, the TOK class, the CAS class, and the Extended Essay class. All subjects and the timetable function will be added at the beginning of next week.

Please bring your current DP subject choice list with you in the welcome week. I have received mulitple emails with requests to change levels and subjects during the break and I tried my best to include them all into my subject choice document. However, I want to go through each subject choice with every student to check that we fulfil all requirements for IB (and also German universities) and to ensure that you will be enrolled into the correct classes. In addition, please bring a laptop or tablet (and a charger), so that you can participate properly.

I am looking forward to meeting you all in the course of next week. Please be in front of the school on Monday by 7:55. Ms. Meraviglia will meet you there and guide you to your classroom. I will check in in the course of the day and introduce you to some basics regarding the IB Diploma Programme and the different types of assessment in this year.

Have a great start everyone!

All the best,

Mr. Metzger (IB Diploma Programme Coordinator)
